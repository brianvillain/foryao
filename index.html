Our Story

So, when you read this, it'll have been nearly three months since we first knew each other, right?

Yeah, I guess you know I was like all the other crypto bros who were just there for the airdrop, heh—at least before I knew you would happen. Hell, before you, I never even watched anyone streaming themselves playing games—lol, I’d rather play them myself. But to be honest, I already liked your voice the moment I heard it. I remember that day—I only tipped you 18,888 ZTX, which was worth around $10 at the time.

Anyway, the next day, November 22, I didn’t know what hit me. I checked Creator’s server and saw your post-stream thank-you message. Normally, I didn’t use Discord much and rarely checked it unless I needed info about some project. But your message? It was very, very genuine. From that moment on… maybe I already had a feeling you were different. You were genuinely grateful—even though my tip wasn’t that big, and even though I stayed throughout your stream, I barely talked. Yeah, but you made a whole photo with my name on it, and in your next stream, you even gave me a custom sticker—meanwhile I knew you had tippers who’d given way more before me.

I gotta admit—I didn’t expect to catch feelings for someone this fast, without even knowing her real name, her face, her age… or anything personal yet. But the things you did hit me differently. That’s why I’ve kept coming back, again and again, for more.
